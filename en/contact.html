Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I come in without an appointment?

To make sure everyone gets timely, unhurried care, we work by appointment. If you're wondering about a same-day opening, just ask us on WhatsApp.

Why don't I see prices on the site?

Prices can vary depending on the treatment and your needs, so the most current rates are available through our booking system — or you can simply ask us directly.

Which language can I write to you in?

You can write to us in Turkish, English, Spanish, German or Russian — our team will reply in that language.

What if I need to reschedule my appointment?

Just message us on WhatsApp and we'll find a new time that works for you.
